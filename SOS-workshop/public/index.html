Communication Technologies for Social Good

SOS site visit workshop at Amsterdam Science Park

On Tuesday November 17th, Stichting Open Spraaktechnologie is visiting Amsterdam Science Park! For this site visit workshop, we have been inspired by the Language Sciences for Social Good consortium at University of Amsterdam, and we are excited to learn about applications of speech and language technologies with societal value.

We are currently collecting talk, poster and demo contributions. Sign up by November 1st through our if you would like to join!

Currently confirmed presentations are listed in our preliminary programme below, we will add more information soon.

Time

Between 14:00 and 18:00

Venue

SustainaLab event space, Amsterdam Science Park campus (location)

Local organizers
